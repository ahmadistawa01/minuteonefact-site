One Minute, One Fact

One-minute factual stories, checked before they are told.

One Minute, One Fact is a YouTube channel of short factual videos. Each one takes a single claim people repeat — that pandemics arrive every hundred years, that the Moon landings were staged — and follows it to what the record actually says.

Watch the channel at youtube.com/@MinuteOneFact.

The uploader

The videos are animated from data rather than filmed, and a small command-line tool publishes the finished file to the channel using the YouTube Data API. It is run by hand, from one person's own computer, and it posts only to the channel that person owns.

It is not a product, a service, or something other people sign into. There are no accounts and no users beyond the channel's owner. What the tool does with the single Google account it touches is set out in full in the privacy policy.
